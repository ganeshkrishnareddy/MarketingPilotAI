1. Executive Summary

MarketingPilot AI is positioned as a centralized AI marketing and automation platform that combines content creation, lead handling, CRM actions, email workflows, WhatsApp workflows, SEO support, ad assistance, and business automation in one product.

The starter phase creates a polished and trustworthy foundation. It covers the first visible product shell, brand presentation, core workflows, and the infrastructure needed to prove the concept before the full production version is funded.

ItemSummary
Project overviewAll-in-one SaaS for marketing operations, lead nurturing, and AI-assisted execution.
ObjectivesLaunch a credible starter release, validate the platform, and prepare for scale.
ScopeLanding page, login, admin shell, lead import, template library, integrations, and starter AI workflows.
DeliverablesTechnical report, system architecture, infrastructure plan, cost sheet, timeline, and launch assumptions.

12. Assumptions & Exclusions

IncludedOptional / not included yet

13. Conclusion

MarketingPilot AI can be launched as a polished starter product within a lean budget if the first release stays focused on the core user journey and the infrastructure remains intentionally lightweight. The platform is designed to scale later into a complete AI marketing suite with CRM, automation, ads, SEO, and communication workflows.

This report gives a client or investor clear visibility into the architecture, technology stack, deployment path, operational cost, and growth roadmap.
